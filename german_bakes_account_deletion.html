Account Deletion

Effective Date: November 3, 2026

German Bakes respects your privacy and gives users the option to request deletion of their accounts and associated personal information.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

If you wish to delete your German Bakes account, you can submit a deletion request using either of the following methods:

Option 1: Through Customer Support

  1. Send an email to our official support email address.
  2. Mention “Account Deletion Request” in the subject line.
  3. Provide your registered mobile number or email address.
  4. Our support team will verify your request and initiate the deletion process.

Option 2: Through the Mobile Application

If an account deletion option is available within the application:

  1. Open the German Bakes application.
  2. Navigate to your Profile or Account Settings.
  3. Select the Delete Account option.
  4. Confirm your account deletion request.

2. What Information Will Be Deleted?

Once your account deletion request is verified and processed, we will delete or anonymize applicable personal information, including:

  • Customer name and profile information.
  • Registered mobile number and email address.
  • Saved delivery addresses.
  • Account login information.
  • Personal account preferences.
  • Other personal information associated with your account, where applicable.

3. Information That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained for a limited period where necessary for:

  • Legal and regulatory compliance.
  • Tax and accounting requirements.
  • Order and transaction records.
  • Fraud prevention and dispute resolution.

Any retained information will be handled securely and only for the purposes permitted by applicable law.

4. Account Deletion Processing Time

We aim to process verified account deletion requests within 30 days.

If additional time is required due to legal or technical reasons, we will communicate the relevant information to the user.

5. Important Information

  • Account deletion is permanent once completed.
  • You may lose access to your order history and saved account information.
  • Deleting the application from your device does not automatically delete your account.
  • Uninstalling the app is not considered an account deletion request.
